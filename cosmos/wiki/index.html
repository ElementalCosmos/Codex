Foundations

Oneness

Oneness is undifferentiated Being. The Codex begins here with non-duality.

Duality

With two beings, there is a relationship between them. Each being also has internal relationality.

Being

Being is undifferentiated in Oneness. With Duality, distinct beings can stand in relationship.

Relationship

Relationship arises between distinct beings. A being can also have internal relationality.

States of Duality

The element grid uses Rational and Arational as one of its two axes.

Perspectives

The perspectives are rationally founded. Relationship is primary and being is secondary.

Elements

The four elements are founded in being. Each being has internal relationality.

Elemental Theory

Elemental Theory is a theory of knowledge of four elements of being by three perspectives of relationship.

Theory of knowledge

This branch asks how we come to know something and how our thinking changes when we look at a different scale.

ÆlementalRadix

The ÆRadix is a circle of twelve elemental sectors. Three perspective sectors, I, We, and All, each contain four elemental sectors. Black lines mark the boundaries between perspectives. Fire and Water use dot marks. Air and Earth use line marks.

Ælemental T’ai Ji Tu

ÆlementalCross

The ÆlementalCross places Fire red above, Water blue below, and Earth ochre extending to both sides.

Zod diagram

The Zod diagram shows how Fire draws on the context of the past and the possibilities of the future to create Earth in the present.

Astrology

Elemental Astrology

Elemental Astrology is one application of Elemental Theory. It relates physical celestial cycles studied in astronomy to metaphysical archetypes.

Perspectives

All → I

A whole considered as All at one scale can be considered as I at another. What appears as one being at the larger scale can also be examined as a whole at its own scale.

I

A being considered as one.

We

Distinct beings considered in relation.

All

The encompassing whole. That whole can be considered as an I at another level.
