Ælemental Method

Basically, you put something in the elemental method and it dissects it, and it shows you what all the parts are and where all the parts are located, and it shows you where you have missing parts, where you have overlapping parts, and where you have gaps.

When you apply the elemental method to a problem or to an object, then it's going to show you where all the gaps and overlaps are.

And that's going to allow you to trim the overlaps and fill in the gaps.

And so it turns your object into a much more full, robust, coherent object.

So it's good for improving what's already been created, but you can also use it as you create.

But, I mean, if I was going to make something, an outline, I could start with the elemental method to do my outline.

I could just begin with the elemental outline and start there.
