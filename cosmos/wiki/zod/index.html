Zod diagram

The Zod diagram shows how Fire draws on the context of the past and the possibilities of the future to create Earth in the present.

Water comes in from the left and represents everything in the past that led to this moment. Air comes in from the right and represents what can become. Fire points down, choosing among the possibilities of Air in the context of Water and making Earth happen.

I, We, and All

Read each cone from its narrow point at the center to its wide end. I is at the narrow point, We lies between I and All, and All is at the wide end, farthest from the center.
