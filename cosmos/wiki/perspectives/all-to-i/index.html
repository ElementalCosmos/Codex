Perspectives

All → I

A whole considered as All at one scale can be considered as I at another. What appears as one being at the larger scale can also be examined as a whole at its own scale.

A cell is an I when considered as a single being. It is part of an organ, which can be considered as All in relation to its cells, and as I in relation to the body.
