Elemental Astrology

Elemental Astrology is one application of Elemental Theory. It relates physical celestial cycles studied in astronomy to metaphysical archetypes.

Consider Mercury. Astronomy describes the physical planet and its orbit. In Elemental Astrology, that orbit can serve as a sign of a process associated with the Mercurial archetype. The archetype can also manifest independently of the planetary cycle.

Cycles and resonance

One hypothesis is that recurring physical patterns create resonance that can influence later patterns. Planetary cycles are predictable enough to use as a reference when studying that possibility. The broader philosophical question is how we recognize and gain agency over recurring patterns in our own thinking.
