States of Duality

The element grid uses Rational and Arational as one of its two axes.

The difference concerns how individual beings are related.

Rational

1 + 1 = 2 expresses a properly structured relationship.

Arational

1, 2, +, 3, = lists individual beings, each taken on its own. The commas keep the signs separate. The list authors no equation relating them.

Irrational

1 + 1 = 3 asserts a relationship with faulty logical structure.
