ÆlementalRadix

The ÆRadix is a circle of twelve elemental sectors. Three perspective sectors, I, We, and All, each contain four elemental sectors. Black lines mark the boundaries between perspectives. Fire and Water use dot marks. Air and Earth use line marks.

The ÆlementalHedron is the three-dimensional dodecahedron. Its twelve faces show the element and perspective combinations.

Fire and the perspectives

The perspectives enter the elements via 🜂 Fire. Fire as essence, or Arational Yang, is where each perspective begins in the ÆRadix.
